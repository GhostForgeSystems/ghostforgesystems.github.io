GhostForge Systems / Sovereignty

Design principles

Sovereignty means authority remains attributable.

In GhostForge products, sovereignty is not a claim that software is independent of every external service. It means ownership, control boundaries and data flows are explicit enough for the operator to understand what is theirs and what is delegated.

01

Owner authority

Root control is established around the human operator rather than inferred from model behavior.

02

Policy before side effects

Consequential capabilities can be gated by explicit authorization rather than natural-language intent alone.

03

Continuity portability

Durable personal state should have a recovery path that is not identical to copying authority credentials.

04

Model independence

Replacing the model should not require replacing the owner's persistent AI system.

05

Auditability

Important control decisions should leave inspectable evidence wherever practical.

06

Truthful boundaries

Local processing, remote providers and external transports are described as different trust boundaries.
