The problem

Your AI relationship is becoming valuable.

A capable personal assistant can learn years of your preferences, goals, history and working context.

If all of that continuity is inseparable from one model provider, changing providers can mean changing assistants.

Infinity is designed to separate the assistant from the engine.

Privacy boundary

Local when you want it. Remote when you choose it.

Infinity can keep inference local when configured with compatible local services. When a remote model or speech provider is selected, the relevant prompt or audio is sent to that provider. Infinity does not pretend those two modes have the same privacy boundary.

Telegram remains an optional separate cloud transport and is not required by Infinity Private Desktop. Public distribution of the final desktop release also requires publisher signing/notarization credentials for macOS and code signing for Windows.

Control

Intelligence is not authority.

Infinity is built around a simple rule: reasoning may propose actions, but protected authority is handled separately.

The desktop interface does not directly own encrypted vault state or authority credentials. Higher-impact control paths remain behind the authority boundary.

This allows the intelligence layer to improve without automatically expanding what it is allowed to control.
