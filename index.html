Why it matters

Capability without ownership creates dependency.

Most AI products make the service provider the natural home of identity, memory and intelligence at the same time. GhostForge separates those concerns.

The goal is not to make AI less capable. It is to let capability improve without silently transferring more authority away from the human using it.
