GhostForge Systems / Technology

Governance architecture

Intelligence should be powerful.
Authority should be explicit.

GhostForge is the control architecture beneath our products: a separation between what AI can reason about and what it is authorized to control.

The model

Four separate layers.

01

Identity

The human or principal that establishes ownership and root authority.

02

Continuity

Durable personal or product state that should not depend on one model vendor.

03

Authority

Capabilities, approvals, locks and evidence paths around consequential actions.

04

Intelligence

Replaceable reasoning models that operate inside the boundaries defined above.

Design rule

“Intelligence may propose. It may not authorize itself.”

Modern models are rapidly improving at reasoning, planning and tool use. That does not require the model itself to become the root of trust.

GhostForge keeps authority-bearing functions in a separate control path so that model upgrades do not silently rewrite ownership, permission or protected-state boundaries.

Technical principles

Built for inspectable control.

01

Detached authority

Protected control functions live outside the replaceable reasoning layer.

02

Scoped capability

Actions can be constrained by scope, time, identity and explicit authorization.

03

Integrity verification

Protected source and governance contracts can be sealed and verified before operation.

04

Evidence paths

Critical authority decisions can emit durable audit evidence instead of relying on opaque model intent.

05

Adapter boundaries

Transport, model providers and product interfaces should not own core authority.

06

Recovery as architecture

Continuity and ownership are designed with explicit restore, lock and reset semantics.

Lineage

From Sovereign to GhostForge.

The current architecture evolved from the Alllgo Sovereign line: a personal assistant concept built around operator loyalty, persistent memory, personas and controlled autonomy. GhostForge hardened that idea into a clearer separation of constitutional authority, platform adapters and runtime capability.
